## About the SIM Lab

The **SIM Lab** (Social & Integrative Media Lab) at SIUE supports research and teaching in Mass Communications. Our projects span course delivery platforms, equipment management tools, open educational resources, and data-driven dashboards.

For questions or collaborations, visit our organization page at [github.com/SIM-Lab-SIUE](https://github.com/SIM-Lab-SIUE).
